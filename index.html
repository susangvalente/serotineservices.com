The person you call when it needs to thrive

Some programs get stuck. The technology exists, but nobody trusts it. The team is capable, but nobody has given them a clear path. That is where I do my best work: not just stabilizing what's broken, but building the conditions for a program to keep getting better after I leave.

I work at the intersection of technology and people, which means I can read a technical schema and run a stakeholder briefing in the same afternoon. As Lead COR, I manage a $200M+ portfolio across nine contracts, including Data.gov, the PX Contact Center, Search.gov, and Vote.gov. Over 22+ years, I have led platform modernizations serving millions of users, rebuilt broken programs during government-wide crises, and briefed senior federal executives on standards I helped build.

This is not limited to government work performed directly. I also partner as a subcontractor on larger federal contract vehicles, bringing deep program and acquisition expertise to primes who need it. And I've helped private business owners in massage therapy, native plant landscaping, and specialty retail get their operations working, without needing to be an expert in their field. I figure out what is needed, build it, and make sure it keeps running after I leave.
